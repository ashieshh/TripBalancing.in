TripBalancing Step 5B - Account Region Pricing

What changed:
- Added Country / Pricing Region to Create Account.
- New accounts choose India or Outside India during registration.
- New-account auth metadata is stored into user_profiles by the Supabase trigger.
- Existing accounts with no region are asked once in the Premium screen.
- Premium pricing is now locked to the saved account region:
  India -> INR prices
  International -> USD prices
- Removed the user-facing manual INR/USD switch after region is saved.
- Razorpay order creation now derives currency server-side from user_profiles.
- Payment verification re-checks that the Razorpay order currency matches the account region.
- International USD payment failure no longer silently falls back to cheaper India pricing.
- Premium modal responsiveness improved: larger desktop width and internal vertical scrolling.

Required database migration:
Run STEP5B_ACCOUNT_REGION_PRICING.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor after deploying this code.

Existing users:
Existing accounts have no region yet. On first opening Premium they will be asked to choose India or Outside India once. The region is then locked in the profile.

New users:
Email/password signup records the chosen region immediately. OAuth/Google users complete it once when opening Premium if no region exists.
