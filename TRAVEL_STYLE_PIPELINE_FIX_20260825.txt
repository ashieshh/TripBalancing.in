TripBalancing Travel Style Pipeline Fix — 2026-08-25

Problem confirmed from live PDF A/B tests:
- Both Couple + Budget and Couple + Luxury exports showed TRAVEL STYLE: BUDGET.
- Root cause: when "Recommend the ideal budget" was selected, the server entered AI Budget Planner mode and hard-coded the Gemini prompt to "Travel Style: Budget (Optimized by AI Budget Planner)".
- That branch also forced economy/budget dining and economy-class cost instructions regardless of the selected Travel Style.

Fixes:
1. AI Budget Planner now preserves the actual selected travelStyle from the form.
2. The same explicit style rules are used in both fixed-budget and AI-recommended-budget modes.
3. AI Budget Planner costs/activities/lodging must follow the selected style instead of silently reverting to Budget.
4. The submitted travelStyle is authoritative after Gemini returns JSON, so Gemini cannot rewrite Luxury/Adventure/etc. to Budget.
5. Cached itinerary responses also re-apply the submitted travelStyle.
6. Traveler-type personalization is unchanged.

Expected A/B test:
- Couple + Budget + Recommend ideal budget -> PDF must say BUDGET and show budget-oriented stays/food/transport.
- Couple + Luxury + Recommend ideal budget -> PDF must say LUXURY and show premium stays/experiences/transport with a higher realistic recommended budget.
- The route/dates/traveler count can remain identical for a fair comparison.

No database/SQL change is required.
