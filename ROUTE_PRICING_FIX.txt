ROUTE PRICING FIX - 2026-08-18

- Flight/transit baseline now depends on origin -> destination route band, not destination cost tier.
- Same-country routes use domestic baseline.
- Same-region international routes use short-haul baseline.
- Nearby-region routes use medium-haul baseline.
- Cross-region routes use long-haul baseline.
- Dubai -> Baku is classified as short-haul international instead of long-haul/high-cost.
- Number of travelers is applied exactly once after the per-person round-trip fare baseline.
- Currency remains display-only and continues to use the shared live FX table.
- No passport/nationality-specific visa/document adjustment was added.
