TRIPBALANCING — ITINERARY QUALITY & REALISM PHASE

Implemented globally without changing the frozen pricing/currency engine:
- Geographic day grouping instruction to reduce cross-city backtracking.
- No repeated major attraction across multiple days.
- Realistic visit/transfer/buffer timing and no overlapping schedule instruction.
- Lighter arrival/departure-day planning where appropriate.
- Opening-hours safety: no fabricated exact live hours; daytime scheduling + verify-live-hours guidance.
- Hotel/restaurant integrity: no claims of guaranteed live availability/current exact rating/current exact price.
- Weather integrity: itinerary generation must not fabricate exact future forecasts; dedicated weather service remains source for forecast data.
- Deterministic post-generation duplicate activity removal across the full trip.
- If AI returns fewer days than requested, missing days are now safe flexible discovery/departure days instead of duplicated copies of the previous day.

Pricing/currency calculations were intentionally left unchanged.
