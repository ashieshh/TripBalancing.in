TripBalancing country selection correction - 2026-08-25

What changed:
- Signup now shows a real country list (ISO country names) instead of "India" vs "Outside India / International".
- The user's actual 2-letter country code is saved to country_code.
- Pricing remains automatic and simple:
    India (IN) => INR pricing
    Every other country => USD pricing
- Existing accounts without a country can select their real country once from the Premium page.
- The Premium page reads the saved account country and shows INR for India, USD for every other country.
- Users do not choose their pricing currency manually.
- Razorpay/server-side plan currency remains derived from the saved account pricing region.

No pricing amounts, plan benefits, UI theme, budget engine, PDF logic, or payment concept were changed.
