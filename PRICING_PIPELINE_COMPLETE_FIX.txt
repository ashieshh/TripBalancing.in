TripBalancing pricing pipeline complete fix - 2026-08-18

1. Currency remains display-only; canonical trip economics are calculated once before FX conversion.
2. International visa/document fees are no longer guessed from destination tier. A modest generic travel-protection/admin allowance is used instead (INR 1,200 per traveler), with no nationality/passport-specific adjustment.
3. Day budgets now represent destination spend only: accommodation + food + local transport + activities + miscellaneous. Flight/intercity travel and trip-level protection stay in the trip summary and are no longer dumped into Day 1.
4. PDF day-budget calculation follows the same rule as the application budget calculator.
5. PDF restaurant/food price ranges no longer impose an INR-sized minimum (150) after conversion. Minimums and rounding now work correctly for currencies such as AED as well as INR.
6. Existing canonical normalization for activity prices, hotel rates, food budget guides, transport fares, attraction fees and local-food price hints remains enabled.

No UI redesign was made.
